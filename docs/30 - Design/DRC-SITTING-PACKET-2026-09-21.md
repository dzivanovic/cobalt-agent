## 4. The decisions

Blocking the build come first. Answer each with the letter or the word.

**Blocking the build**

3. The DRC stays its own note in the Review folder, one per day, as today? Yes, or B, it moves inside the daily note. B undoes the 3:40 writer, the evening miss-line placement and the morning reader.

4. Which sections are yours alone, never touched by Cobalt? Say yes to this list or change it: Goal and Grade, How I managed risk, Technology, Collaboration, both What I learned sections, Selectivity notes, Tomorrow's one percent better. Everything you name here Cobalt only ever reads.

10. What should the 9:00 proposal quote from yesterday's DRC? A, grade only, as today. B, grade and tomorrow's one percent If and Then. C, grade and your goal. B or C costs a new reader tested on your template's real shape.

11. Your self-grade: is it always one of A plus, A, B or C? Yes, or no and name the others. Today any other value reads as not filled, and the morning proposal steps down a rung.

12. The three-line stub in the daily note: keep it, or drop it? If kept, where: A, under Notes, next to Overall Score, or B, at the end of the note.
