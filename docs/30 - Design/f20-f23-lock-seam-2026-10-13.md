1. **Merge order.** The card that merges to `main` first lands as built. The card that merges second rebases onto the first's `pyproject.toml`, keeping its one pin line, and its `uv.lock` is regenerated (rule 2), never merged. Neither card names the order; the desk sets it at the first deploy card. The rebase is the desk's: `git rebase` and `git merge` are never typed by a builder (`BUILD-HUB.md:14`).
2. **Who runs `uv lock`.**
