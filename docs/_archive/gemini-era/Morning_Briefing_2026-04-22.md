### General Market Tone

**Mood:** Cautiously Bullish 
The broader market is pointing to a positive open as investors embrace an indefinite extension of the U.S.-Iran ceasefire. While peak uncertainty seems to have passed, sentiment remains somewhat fragile as oil prices hover near $100 a barrel due to ongoing shipping disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. Earnings optimism and resilient consumer data are providing an underlying bid to equities.

**Internals:** 
The VIX is currently trading around **18.30**, up roughly 4.39% on the session, indicating that while systemic panic has subsided (remaining below the key 20 level), market participants are still pricing in active risk and hedging against potential weekend/geopolitical headline shocks.

**Economic / Macro Backdrop:**
*   **Geopolitics:** President Donald Trump announced an indefinite extension to the U.S.-Iran ceasefire. However, the Strait of Hormuz remains mostly closed, locking in supply disruptions and keeping crude oil prices elevated. 
*   **Economic Data:** U.S. retail sales for March 2026 came in hot, rising 1.7% (beating the 1.4% forecast), heavily driven by higher gasoline prices. 
*   **Yields:** Treasury yields have ticked higher on the oil price surge and recent Senate nomination hearings, with the 10-year yield touching 4.31% before pulling back slightly.

**Day-Trading Implication:**
Expect a "buy-the-dip" mentality in mega-cap tech and AI-linked names, which remain insulated from direct commodity shocks. However, the elevated VIX and high oil prices mean intraday trend reversals are highly probable. Focus on relative strength in software/tech and potential short setups in companies directly impacted by supply chain or margin pressures.
