ASET Framework

A systematic trade-structuring discipline. Every position sized by math — not emotion.

A · Allocation S · Stop E · Entry T · Target
A
Step 1

Allocation

Score the trade by catalyst, setup and trade quality. Grade determines how much of your daily risk budget this trade deserves.

  • Catalyst — is there a meaningful reason?
  • Setup — recognized pattern?
  • Trade — sector/regime/relative volume confirming?
S
Step 2

Stop

The price level where the thesis is proven wrong. Structure first, feelings never.

  • Range-break trade: below range low.
  • Consolidation trade: below bound.
  • Never skip pre-defined risk.
E
Step 3

Entry

A precise trigger — price level plus confirmation — that turns an idea into a real trade.

  • Break close above/below level.
  • Pullback reclaim/reject.
  • Continuation after confirmation.
T
Step 4

Target

Define the reward side. Minimum 2-to-1 R:R. Target can be adjusted only when the structure supports it.

  • 1R: measured move / first structure.
  • 2R: extended move with momentum.
  • No target = no trade.
