## V3-2 (replaces V2-2) — archive progress, independent of `max(ts)` · takes Astra + Gemini; Grok accepted V2-2 but filed the same fact under V2-7
`BarStore.watermark()` stays for the poller and for reporting. NEW additive table `system.archive_progress`: `(ticker, interval) PK, archived_through timestamptz, fetch_started_at, export_oldest, export_newest, run_id, updated_at` — written ONLY by the archiver, atomically with the target's inserts and outcome. It records the boundary through which an accepted export was processed; it does not certify that the vendor supplied every bar. The candidate range of a target = every COMPLETE bar (V2-3) of the download that lies in the UNION of (a) the unprocessed range `ts > archived_through` and (b) the two most recent COMPLETED trading sessions at fetch start plus the current day — sessions being America/New_York calendar dates that carry bars in the export (no fixed 24-hour arithmetic; a weekend or holiday simply has no date in the export). NO PROGRESS ROW (first run after the deploy, a new target) → an explicit BOOTSTRAP: the whole available export is reconciled once (compare + DO NOTHING), regardless of rows the poller already stored. That first night costs about one old-style night of key probes WITHOUT rewriting a row; it is stated in the run report as `bootstrap`. Progress never advances on a failed, withheld or empty target.
To Grok, who refused any side table in round 1: `max(ts)` cannot carry this — your own V2-7 sequence shows it — and the table holds one row per target (≈1,000 rows), logical keys only, nothing about the physical layout of `bars`.
