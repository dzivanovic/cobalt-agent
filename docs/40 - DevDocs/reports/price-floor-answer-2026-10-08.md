# $5 price floor (his R692): design and card brief · brain · 2026-10-08

## §0 Headline
- **One seam:** `RadarRunner._collect` (`src/cobalt/radar/runner.py:103-172`). Every screen and list row is read there before anything is ranked, admitted or carded. A row whose price is at or below the floor never becomes a candidate, never enters a SourceSet's tickers, and so never reaches the pool, departed, excluded, cards or sheets.
- **Price:** the export's `Price` column in that scan, re-read every 3-minute cycle. "At the moment" means the latest scan. **Removed: price ≤ 5.00; kept: > 5.00.** His operative sentence was "remove every stock of $5 and below".
- **Open cards:** a WATCH card goes to EXPIRED (reason `price floor`). ARMED, TRIGGERED and FILLED cards are never touched: a risk commitment or a position is his.
- **Tunable:** `radar.price_floor` in `"user".trader_settings` (L69), default `5.00`. A missing row refuses loud, as `radar.cards_enabled` does.
- **One new card** (new scope, not a finding on 118 or 134). It does not edit `radar_panel.py`, so it does not conflict with 118 or 134. It deploys alone, before his S3 smoke.

## THE CARD BRIEF (for the drafter)
- **F1 · price at collect** (`runner.py` `_collect`): read `Price` for every row (add `Price` to `export.required_headers` in `configs/cobalt/radar.yaml`, and add a `price` metric). A row whose price is ≤ the floor is skipped: not added to `tickers`, `metrics` or `candidates`, and counted per source in the cycle log (`price floor: <n> removed (<tickers>)`). A row with no readable price is kept, flagged `price_unknown` in its metrics and logged once per ticker per day. Unknown is not below. The drafter proves with a grep that every source export (screens AND `listed` lists) carries `Price`. A source without it is named in the card, and its rows get the latest stored bar close for that ticker; with no price either way, they are kept and flagged.
- **F2 · members that cross under:** an admitted member whose price drops ≤ the floor drops out of the candidates, so it departs through the normal membership path, with departure reason `price_floor` (a new `ExcludedBy`/departure value in `radar/models.py`). The departed and excluded reads in `radar/store.py` leave out `price_floor` rows, so they appear in no list on any page. The data keeps them for the DRC. Each scan re-reads the price, so a name back above the floor is a fresh candidate (its normal re-admit rules apply).
- **F3 · WATCH cards:** in the same cycle, a radar card in WATCH whose ticker was removed by F1 or F2 moves WATCH → EXPIRED through `CardStore.transition` (actor cobalt, evidence `{"via":"radar.price_floor","price":…}`). Only ARMED, TRIGGERED and FILLED are left as they are. Manual sheet cards he typed are not touched.
- **F4 · the tunable:** `radar.price_floor` (Decimal, default `5.00`) in the reviewed card-settings file, loaded by the deploy with the existing `cobalt settings load --card … --sha256 … --apply` step. The runner reads it each cycle through `TraderSettings`. A missing row means `CardSettingsError`, the cycle evaluates nothing, and the error is named (as `cards_enabled` does today).
- **Reds:** (a) a screen row at 4.99 and at 5.00 appears in the pool on BASE and is absent after; 5.01 stays. (b) An admitted member falling to 4.80 departs with `price_floor` and is absent from the departed read. (c) A WATCH card on a ticker that crosses under is EXPIRED, and an ARMED one is untouched. (d) No `price_floor` row means a loud refusal. (e) A row without `Price` is kept and flagged.
- **Fence:** `radar/runner.py`, `radar/models.py`, `radar/store.py` (departed and excluded reads only), `configs/cobalt/radar.yaml`, the card-settings file, and tests. NOT `aset/radar_panel.py`. DB: with-DB tests, no migration if the departure reason is a text value. If the drafter finds that the reason column has a CHECK constraint, the card gets the migration (then its own card, per ROW A).
