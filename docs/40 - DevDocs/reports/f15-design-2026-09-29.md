## DIGEST FOR THE DESK
WHAT IS NEEDED
- Every grade a radar card shows must be recomputable from what was stored, and each grade must be joined to how the trade ended.
- Today the scan grades already are recomputable. A dot tap is not: it changes the grade, but neither the result nor the key bands it used are kept.

WHERE IT FITS
- It sits after S3 exits and the DRC, in the same deploy.
- Migration 0022, after S3's 0021 and the DRC's 0016 and 0018–0020. It lands last in the stacked gate.

HOW
- Every time the card's grade is written (card created, each scan, each tap), Cobalt appends one row it can never edit.
- A scan row points at the scan's stored inputs. A tap row keeps its own inputs.
- `cobalt cards replay <card>` recomputes each row with the same scorer version and prints MATCH, DIFF or NOT REPLAYABLE with the reason, plus the outcome (legs, realized R, provisional or not, the missed counterfactual).
- `cobalt cards corpus` lists every card's grade next to its outcome and shows the count first.

- P1 closes the first four. The fifth is exposed by replay's ROW line but not fixed. For a card graded before F15, replay says so.
